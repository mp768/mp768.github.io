Welcome to my game development and prototyping portfolio. Both projects below were built in Godot 4.7 (Mono) with C#. There are no web builds, so grab the Windows or Mac download to play.

Project 2: Machine Cards

Technologies: Godot 4.7 (Mono), C#

Description: A factory-building card game prototype. You lay cards out in a row and materials flow through the line from left to right. Each card transforms the resources that reach it, consuming some and producing others, so the order of the cards is the whole puzzle. You get a few seconds to build a production line, then watch it run and hope the right product comes out the other end.

My Contributions: Made solo as a prototype. I did all of the programming and all of the art.
